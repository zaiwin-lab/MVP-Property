# PRODUCT.md — KB Kuching Top Apartments

## Register
brand

## Product
AI-powered property concierge platform for Kuching's premium apartment market. Not a listing portal — an exclusive lead-generation and appointment-booking platform for up to 10 Founding Property Partners. Each partner gets a personal page (/fizan, /amin, /raju) with their own AI assistant, calculators, and appointment booking.

## Target Users
- **Buyers**: Kuching residents or investors researching premium apartments (Riverine, SkyVilla, Milano Eight). Age 28–55. Mobile-first. Considering RM 390k–550k purchases.
- **Property Partners**: Agents/negotiators who pay RM3,000/year to get their own concierge page, leads, and introducer network.
- **Introducers**: Referrers who share links (/fizan?ref=john) and track their referred leads.

## Purpose
Convert property browsers into booked appointments with trusted partners. The platform must build immediate trust, showcase lifestyle aspiration, and make the next step (chat, calculate, book) feel effortless.

## Emotional Target
Confidence, aspiration, exclusivity, trust. The visitor should feel: "This is the premium way to buy property in Kuching."

## Brand Personality
Exclusive · Cinematic · Trustworthy

## Anti-References
- PropertyGuru (cluttered listing portal)
- iProperty (marketplace feel, too much info density)
- Generic real-estate CRM tools
- Corporate dark SaaS dashboards

## Aspirational References
- Sotheby's International Realty (editorial luxury)
- One&Only / Aman Resorts (cinematic minimalism)
- Apple product pages (purposeful motion, hero depth)
- Knight Frank (authoritative, premium typographic hierarchy)

## Color System
Dark navy (#0a1628) as primary surface. Gold (#c9a84c → #e4c97e gradient range) as the single accent. White for primary text. Committed dark color strategy — the depth IS the brand.

## Typography
- Display/headings: Playfair Display (serif, italic for hero accent)
- Body/UI: Geist Sans
- Target feel: editorial magazine meets luxury hotel concierge

## Key Surfaces
- `/` — Homepage: hero, apartment showcase, video, calculators, AI CTA, appointment form
- `/[slug]` — Partner page: personalized concierge with referral tracking
- `/admin` — KOBIS super admin dashboard
- `/dashboard` — Partner dashboard

## Accessibility
Standard WCAG AA. All text on dark navy must hit 4.5:1 contrast.

## Design Principles
1. Every section earns the scroll — no filler
2. Gold is used purposefully, not everywhere
3. Motion enhances narrative, never distracts
4. The AI assistant is the hero product — surface it prominently
5. Premium spacing: breathe, don't cram
